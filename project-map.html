What degrades gracefully

  • AI dies → the offline editor (structureStoryDraft) still shapes a page from the user's own words. Quality drops; the product works.
  • Supabase pauses → the journal keeps working on the device. Only sign-in and cross-device sync stop.
  • Google sign-in blocked → people can still write without an account. It is device-first by design.
  • A bad deploy → Vercel can roll back to the last good one in seconds.

So a bad hour is a bad hour, not a catastrophe. The one thing that fails silently is the AI key — the site looks fine and quietly stops being itself.
