## The ask, and why it's this one

Two things you could ask for. **Ask for the second.**

1. ~~Licence the New Zealand sketchbook.~~ Wrong content anyway — it's a travel
   diary about the South Island. Your users write about Amma, Malleswaram
   market, the rain in Cubbon Park. You would be shipping someone else's
   holiday.
2. **Commission a piece in the same craft, for your reader.** She already knows
   how to build the thing you want — paper that feels like paper, pages that
   curl, drawings that look hand-made. Paying her to make one about *your*
   product's world is the honest version of "I want exactly what she made".

Lead with the commission. Mention licensing only as an alternative.

## X DM — the primary (keep it this short)

> Hi Ann — I build a small memoir app called Life on Paper. Someone showed me
> your NZ sketchbook and I've watched the page turn about twenty times. The
> paper tooth under the pencil hatching is the detail I keep coming back to.
>
> I'd love to commission a piece in that same craft for our reader — a
> sketchbook page that curls the way yours does, drawn for our world rather
> than the South Island. Paid properly, credited however you like.
>
> No pressure at all if it's not your thing. Either way, that sketchbook is
> lovely work.

Why this works: specific (proves you actually looked), names the thing you want,
says "paid", offers credit, and gives her an easy way to say no.

## Before you send

- **Be ready to hear no, and be gracious about it.** She made it as a paid
  sponsor piece for TypingMind; she may not be free to reuse or extend it, and
  the sponsor may have terms.
- **Have a budget in mind** before you ask, so you can answer "what's your
  rate?" without going quiet. Even a rough number respects her time.
- **Expect it to take weeks.** This is not a pre-launch task. If she says yes
  after launch, that's still a win.
- **If she never replies:** build your own. The technique isn't owned by
  anyone — paper grain, page curl and hatched brush strokes are general craft.
  Your version, your art, your users' lives. `LAUNCH.md` and the plan on
  `project-map.html` cover where that would land.

## Do not

- Do not say "I want exactly what you made, for my app" — that reads as
  *"I want to take your work"*.
- Do not reuse the file in the meantime and promise to "change the design
  later". Shipping it is shipping it, however temporary you mean it to be.
- Do not drop TypingMind's name as leverage. She was commissioned by them;
  they are not a reference you can borrow.
